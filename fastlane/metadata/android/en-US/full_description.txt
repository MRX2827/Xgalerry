Xgalerry is a local gallery for Android. Browse photos and videos by date, open folders as albums, and find a file by its name. Keep a private list of favorites on your device.

Thumbnails expand into the viewer with a spring animation inspired by AMORX. Swipe sideways to move between files, swipe down to close, or pinch and double tap to zoom into a photo. Videos play in the same viewer with pause, replay and a seek bar.

Choose a light, dark, or system theme. Android 12 and later can use your wallpaper colors. On Android 14 and later, you can choose which photos and videos to share with the gallery.

The app works offline and has no account, ads, or analytics.
